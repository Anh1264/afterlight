00Verdict

Spec criterion 3 sets three bars the levels must clear in the simulator before any human plays them. Each bar is judged on the lower end of the 95% confidence interval, so a lucky run cannot pass.

06Fairness and data checks

Going first and sitting in seat 0 should not decide a pairing. Every recorded match must also replay to the same result from its seed, decks and actions alone.

07What the bots do with their cards

The same starter decks in different hands. "R1 share" is the part of a card's plays made in Round 1. A lower share means the bot keeps that card for later rounds.

Win rate when played is correlational: bots play more cards in matches they are winning. Read it as a hint, not as card power.

08Evaluation

  • The ladder holds where the spec checks it. Elo puts Hard at 1683, Medium at 1500, Easy at 1439 and Random at 1166. Hard beats Medium 89.7% and Medium beats Easy 67.3%, both well clear of their bars. All 2,688 matches replayed to the identical result from their inputs, so the record format is ready for human match data.
  • Easy's failed bar comes mostly from how the test measures it. When Random tries a pass that would lose it the match, the fairness guard swaps in Random's best play instead. That happened in 57 games, and Random went on to win 19 of them and draw 3. Counted as the uniform-random bot the spec describes, Easy scores 83.8% (CI 80.1–86.9%) and passes. Easy does have a real leak: it passes by chance while ahead even in the deciding round. Fixing that leak drops Medium's score against Easy to 61%, below its 65% band. Recommendation: measure against an unguarded Random and leave Easy as it is.
  • Hard's biggest weakness is that its imagined opponent is always Medium. In its imagined games Medium gives up a round that would hand it the match. Easy, Random and humans just take the round. So after losing Round 1, Hard passes early in Round 2 expecting to win. Its losses to Easy follow one pattern: it drops Rounds 1 and 2 (88 of its 140 losses). That pattern never appears against Medium.

    Recommendation: ship Hard as mcts-2 with the mixed imagined opponent, and add a ladder bar that Hard beats Easy at 80% or more.
  • Think time is still unproven. Hard's 95th percentile measured 2.2 s with 9 matches running in parallel, and 2.6 s alone while other jobs held the load average at 20–39. Earlier single-core runs measured 1.37–1.41 s, just inside the 1.5 s budget. BL-2 measures on Railway's CPU. If it's too slow there, 80 iterations is the fallback: 0.74 s p95 and still 82% against Medium.
  • For the designer: Coven is weak in bot hands, scoring 43.0% (CI 40.0–46.1%) over 1,008 games against other houses. The other houses sit at 51–53%. Moving first gives no measurable edge (48.1%, CI 46.2–50.0%).
  • Decisions for Aiden.
    • Random baseline: measure Easy against an unguarded Random? This matches the spec's wording. Recommended: yes.
    • Hard mcts-2: build the mixed-opponent Hard and the Hard-beats-Easy bar before BL-2 (the server work)? Recommended: yes. It's a small engine task.
    • Coven: rebalance it now, or wait for human match data? Recommended: wait. Bot-vs-bot results only say how bots play the house.
