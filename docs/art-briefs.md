# Art briefs

96 cards still need art. Cards without art show their house symbol and **ART PENDING**.

## How to add art

1. Generate the character in the same painterly style as the existing Legends. Make it a full body on a transparent background, portrait about 800×1200.
2. Save it as `client/public/art/<card-id>.png` (`.webp` and `.jpg` also work, but use a transparent PNG or WebP so the background shows through).
3. Commit and push. The build picks up the new file automatically and the card shows its art.

The two coloured afterimage copies behind the character are generated in the browser, so you don't need extra files.

Order of work: Legends first, then Rares, then Commons. Spells (`SPELL:`) are scenes or effects rather than characters.
